## Visual direction from actual footage
- Single compact band made of many independent luminous golden filaments, not one thick flat ribbon.
- Curved S-fold from lower left through a fairly horizontal middle into a steep ascent at upper right.
- Rolling cross-sectional twist: strands converge at pinch points and diverge without breaking their continuous paths.
- Bright near-white highlights, amber/gold cores, warm haze and dark undershading. On the established WHITE glass backdrop, gold must be darker than the original black-background demo to remain visible.
